The same roll-off fleet serves both homes and job sites — but the constraints differ. Typical price ranges by size are covered in [how much dumpster rentals cost](/guides/how-much-do-dumpster-rentals-cost).

## Residential

- Driveway protection and weight on concrete matter
- HOA and neighbor access
- Smaller sizes (10–20 yd) are most common
- Same-day may be limited; plan 1–3 days out
- Cleanouts and remodels dominate

## Commercial / contractor

- Larger sizes (20–40 yd) and multi-box swaps
- Job-site access, staging, and safety
- Account billing and volume pricing
- Concrete-only and continuous service more common
- Permit coordination with GC schedules

## Questions for either

- Who is responsible for permits?
- What is included (days, tons, delivery window)?
- What happens if the load is overweight or rejected?
- Can you protect the surface under the rails?
