Rules vary by landfill, transfer station, and operator. Always confirm with the hauler before loading.

## Commonly accepted

- Construction and demolition debris (wood, drywall, flooring)
- Household junk and furniture (non-hazardous)
- Yard waste (some operators require green-waste-only boxes)
- Roofing shingles (weight-sensitive)
- Mixed debris from remodels

## Often restricted or prohibited

- Hazardous waste (paint solvents, chemicals, oil, asbestos)
- Electronics and appliances with refrigerants (may need special handling)
- Tires and batteries
- Medical waste
- Dirt, concrete, or asphalt in mixed-debris dumpsters (often need dedicated containers)

## Material-specific boxes

Many operators offer **concrete/dirt only** or **yard waste only** containers at different rates. Using the right box can lower cost and avoid rejected loads.

## Before you load

1. Tell the operator what you plan to dispose of.
2. Ask what is banned at their dump facility.
3. Keep hazardous items out of the mix — rejected loads are expensive.
