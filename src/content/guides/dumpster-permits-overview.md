Permit rules are local. City pages on DumpsterLocal summarize common patterns — always verify with your municipality.

## When you usually need a permit

- Dumpster sits on a **public street**, sidewalk, or alley (right-of-way)
- Placement blocks traffic or parking in regulated zones
- Some cities require permits for long rental durations even on private property (less common)

## When you often do not

- Dumpster is fully on **private property** (driveway, parking lot) with owner permission
- HOA/condo rules may still apply even without a city permit

## Who pulls the permit?

- **Operator** pulls it for a fee (convenient; confirm cost)
- **Customer** pulls it (sometimes cheaper; more paperwork)

Clarify this **before delivery**. Street placement without a required permit can mean tickets or forced relocation.

## Tips

- Prefer driveway placement when possible.
- Share photos of the drop location with the operator.
- Check condo board or HOA rules for multi-unit buildings.
- After storms, temporary rules and demand can change quickly.
