# Find A Dumpster

Verified local dumpster rental directory. Seekers request quotes; operators receive held leads after admin routing.

## Language

**Operator**:
A dumpster rental company listed in the directory.
_Avoid_: provider, vendor, company, listing (use “listing” only for the public profile page)

**Seeker**:
A visitor requesting a dumpster quote.
_Avoid_: customer, lead (the person), user, shopper

**Lead**:
One quote request, held until an admin routes it. May be cloned (same group) when offered to more than one operator.
_Avoid_: inquiry, ticket, job

**Direct request**:
A lead submitted on a specific operator’s profile.
_Avoid_: listing lead, single-provider lead

**Match request**:
A lead submitted from search or a city page, not tied to one operator yet.
_Avoid_: marketplace lead, broadcast, RFP

**Offer**:
Admin makes a redacted lead visible to an operator. Contact details stay hidden until unlock.
_Avoid_: assign, send, dispatch (use “route” for the admin action)

**Unlock**:
Reveal seeker contact to an operator, via complimentary credit or paid checkout.
_Avoid_: claim (claim is for listing ownership), purchase (unless referring to Stripe)

**Complimentary**:
The one free unlock each operator starts with.
_Avoid_: free quota (unless talking about a future monthly reset), teaser credit

**Claim**:
An operator asserting ownership of a listing through a Clerk organization. Requires admin approval.
_Avoid_: unlock, verify

**Route**:
Admin choosing which operator(s) receive a held lead.
_Avoid_: auto-match, distribute (until matching is automated)
